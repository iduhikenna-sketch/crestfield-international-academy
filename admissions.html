Welcome to Admissions

A Clear Path Into Crestfield

We aim to make the admissions journey straightforward, informative and supportive for every prospective family.

Crestfield International Academy welcomes families who are looking for a structured, supportive and forward-thinking educational environment for their children.

Our admissions process allows families to learn more about the school, understand the expectations for their child's entry level and complete the necessary steps before enrolment.

Families are encouraged to contact the school before submitting an application so that they can receive the most appropriate guidance for their child's admission stage.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

Admissions are available for students entering different stages of their educational journey.

01

New Students

Families seeking a new school placement may begin the admissions process by submitting an enquiry and learning about the appropriate entry level.

02

Transfer Students

Students transferring from another school can provide relevant academic records so the admissions team can determine the appropriate placement.

03

Different Entry Levels

Applications may be considered across the school's Early Years, Primary, Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary stages.

04

Families Seeking a Strong Partnership

Crestfield values families who are prepared to work together with the school to support the student's academic and personal growth.

Requirements

What You May Need

The following documents may be requested during the admissions process.

Student Information

Basic information about the prospective student's age, previous school and educational history.

Previous Academic Records

Recent report cards, assessment records or other relevant academic documents where applicable.

Birth Documentation

Appropriate documentation confirming the student's identity and date of birth may be requested.

Passport Photograph

Recent passport photographs may be required as part of the student's admission documentation.

Transfer Documents

Transfer students may need to provide additional records from their previous school.

Parent or Guardian Details

Contact and identification information for the parent or legal guardian responsible for the student.

How It Works

Application Process

A simple four-stage process helps families move from enquiry to enrolment.

1

Make an Enquiry

Contact the school and provide basic information about the prospective student.

2

Submit Application

Complete the application process and provide the requested supporting documents.

3

Assessment & Interview

Where applicable, the student may participate in an assessment or age-appropriate interview.

4

Admission & Enrolment

Successful applicants receive admission guidance and complete the final enrolment requirements.

Student Placement

Assessment & Interview

The assessment stage helps the school understand each student's current learning level and educational needs.

A

Age-Appropriate Assessment

Depending on the student's entry level, an age-appropriate assessment may be used to understand existing knowledge and skills.

B

Student Interaction

Students may have an opportunity to interact with members of the school community as part of the placement process.

C

Family Conversation

Parents or guardians may discuss the student's educational background, interests and expectations with the school.

D

Appropriate Placement

Information gathered during the process helps guide the student's appropriate entry and placement within the school.

What Happens Next?

Admission Timeline

The exact timing may vary depending on the admission cycle and availability of places.

STEP 1

Initial Enquiry

Families contact the school to discuss the student's intended entry level and admissions requirements.

STEP 2

Application Review

Submitted information and supporting documents are reviewed by the admissions team.

STEP 3

Assessment

Where required, the student completes the relevant assessment or interview process.

STEP 4

Admission Decision

The family receives information about the outcome and the next steps toward enrolment.

STEP 5

Enrolment

The family completes the final registration and enrolment requirements before the student begins school.

Final Stage

Completing Enrolment

Once admission has been confirmed, families can prepare for the student's official entry into the school community.

Before the student's first day, the school will provide the family with the relevant enrolment information and guidance.

  • Complete required registration documents.
  • Submit any outstanding student records.
  • Confirm the student's admission and placement.
  • Receive information about school expectations and procedures.
  • Complete any applicable financial or administrative requirements.
  • Prepare the student for the start of the school term.
Admissions FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions families may have.

Can I enquire before submitting an application?

Yes. Families are encouraged to contact the school first so they can understand the appropriate admission requirements and process.

Does every applicant take an assessment?

Assessment requirements may vary according to the student's age, entry level and admission stage.

Can transfer students apply?

Yes. Transfer applicants may provide relevant academic and school records as part of the admission process.

What documents should parents prepare?

Families should prepare student identification and educational records, together with any additional documents requested by the admissions team.

How do I begin the admissions process?

Start by contacting Crestfield International Academy through the enquiry option below.
