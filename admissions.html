A Simple, Family-Friendly Admissions Experience

The admissions area is designed to give parents a clear understanding of what happens before a student joins the school.

Families can review requirements, select a programme, submit an enquiry, and receive information about the next stage of the process.

This entire admissions experience is fictional and is included to demonstrate how a modern school's website could work.

How It Works

Four Simple Steps

A sample admissions workflow for the fictional Crestfield Academy.

01

Make an Enquiry

Contact the admissions team and request information about the appropriate programme.

02

Submit Application

Complete the fictional application form with the required student and parent information.

03

Assessment

The fictional school may arrange an age-appropriate assessment or family meeting.

04

Admission Decision

Families receive information about the next stage following the fictional assessment process.

Sample Admission Requirements

These are fictional examples created for the demonstration.

Student Information

  • Student's full name
  • Date of birth
  • Previous school information
  • Intended year or class
  • Recent academic records where applicable

Parent / Guardian Information

  • Parent or guardian's full name
  • Valid contact number
  • Email address where available
  • Residential information
  • Emergency contact information
