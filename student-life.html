Beyond the Classroom

A School Experience That Encourages Participation

School life is more than lessons and examinations. Students benefit from opportunities to collaborate with classmates, discover interests and participate in activities that help them grow as individuals.

Crestfield's student-life programme combines weekly clubs, organised sports and additional activities that encourage communication, discipline, creativity and teamwork.
